PlanetFOSS gathers Linux and open source news from many German and English sources. The app brings planetfoss.net to your phone – native, fast and without an account.

- Latest: every post, with the same story from different sources bundled
- Supernovas: topics many sources are reporting on right now, plus rising topics (comets)
- Notifications for new supernovas if you turn them on – no push server, no Google services
- Rubrics, topics, sources and videos, search with filters
- Save articles and find them again offline
- Content in German, English or both; the app speaks German and English
- Open articles in your own browser (Custom Tab) or in the app's browser, optionally with protection from third-party trackers
- Support the sources: where a publisher offers a subscription or donations, the app takes you there

Fair to the sources: PlanetFOSS only links and shows no full text. The app does not block ads by default and passes PlanetFOSS on as referrer, so publishers see where their readers come from.

Privacy: no account, no ad or analytics services, no Google services. The app only talks to planetfoss.net and the sites you open. If you like, switch on “Count for the sources”: planetfoss.net then counts opened articles as daily totals for the publishers – without any device identifier. It is off by default. Sponsor cards, if PlanetFOSS shows any, are marked as ads and counted anonymously as daily totals, like on the website.

PlanetFOSS for Android is free software under the GNU GPL 3.0 or later. Requires Android 14 or newer.
